Welcome to Pixel Weave Game. This Privacy Policy outlines how VirelonSoft ("Company", "we", "us", or "our") manages and protects the data of our users. By choosing to play Pixel Weave, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information Architecture

At VirelonSoft, we value your privacy. We do not collect personal identifiers such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers. Our data architecture is designed around the local device experience.

Automatically processed data includes:

  • Device Advertising Identifiers (Google Advertising ID).
  • In-game progression telemetry (level completion, accuracy scores).
  • Technical metadata (device model, OS version).

2. Integrated Core Services

Our application integrates specific third-party frameworks to ensure a premium gaming experience:

  • Google AdMob: Facilitates rewarded and interstitial advertisements. They may process your AD_ID to tailor content. View Policy
  • Unity Engine: Powering the weaving mechanics. Unity collects hardware identifiers for optimization. View Policy

3. Local Data Sovereignty

All weaving patterns, stars earned, and game progress are stored exclusively on your device. VirelonSoft does not maintain a central server to store your personal gameplay data.

4. Children's Safety Protocol

Our game is designed with a safe environment in mind. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Pixel Weave is a creativity-focused experience suitable for a wide audience while strictly adhering to COPPA-related guidelines.

5. Advertising Permissions (AD_ID)

Pixel Weave Game utilizes the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID to enable AdMob reward tracking, ensuring you get your hints correctly after watching rewarded content.
